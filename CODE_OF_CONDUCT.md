# Code of Conduct

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Review the code, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback constructively.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new once.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Contact an organization owner privately. Reports are handled confidentially.
